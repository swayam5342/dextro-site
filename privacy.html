dextro

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

dextro is self-hosted software. It runs on a computer or server chosen by the person who installs it, and that person controls the data. dextro doesn't send your data to its developer, and there's no central dextro service.

What dextro accesses from Google

  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and Google account ID, which identify your account in dextro.
  • If you link a Google Drive account: the account's email address and how much storage it has used, plus the files dextro created. dextro uses the drive.file permission, so it can't see anything else in your Drive.

How dextro uses it

dextro uses this information only to sign you in, show you your files, store the files you upload, and choose which linked account has room. It isn't used for advertising, and dextro has no analytics or tracking.

Where it's stored

  • Your files stay in your own Google Drive, in a folder named dextro.
  • Your account details, the names and sizes of your files, and the token that keeps a Drive account linked are stored on the server running dextro. Tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • That server also keeps a security log of actions such as sign-ins, including IP addresses, so its admin can review them.

Who it's shared with

dextro doesn't sell your data or share it with anyone. It sends data only to Google, in order to do what you ask, such as storing a file. The admin of the server you use can see your email address and how many accounts you've linked, but not your files through dextro.

Google API Services

dextro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Removing a linked account on the Storage page deletes its token and dextro's records of its files.
  • The files themselves stay in your Google Drive, where you can delete them yourself.
  • To remove dextro's access on Google's side, go to your Google account's connections.
  • The admin of your server can delete your dextro account.

Contact
