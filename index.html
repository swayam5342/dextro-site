dextro

Your Google Drive accounts, used as one pool of storage

dextro is a self-hosted web app. You run it on your own computer or server, link one or more of your Google Drive accounts, and upload, organize and preview your files in one place.

What it does

  • Sends each upload to the linked account with the most free space, or to the account you pick.
  • Shows your files, folders and storage across every linked account.
  • Resumes large uploads if the connection drops.

How it uses your Google account

dextro asks for the drive.file permission, so it can only reach files it created itself. They sit in a folder named dextro in your Drive. It can't see anything else in your Drive. If you sign in with Google, it also receives your email address and account ID.

Your files stay in your own Google Drive, and everything dextro records stays on the server you run. Read the privacy policy for the details.
